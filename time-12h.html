The two rules

Morning (00:00 – 11:59) keeps the same hour number and takes AM; 00:xx becomes 12:xx AM. Afternoon and night (12:00 – 23:59) take PM and the hour is reduced by 12, so 23:45 becomes 11:45 PM.

12:00 on its own is noon, 00:00 is midnight — both written as 12:00 with their AM/PM label doing the work.
