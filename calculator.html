How percentages behave here

In Standard and Scientific mode 10% simply means 0.10, so 200 + 10% gives 200.1. For “10% of 200”, “50 is what % of 200” and percentage change, use the Percentage tab — it does the right maths and draws the answer as a bar.

Trigonometry follows the DEG / RAD button in Scientific mode. Typing on a keyboard works too: digits, + − * / ^ ( ) %, Enter for =, Backspace to erase, Esc to clear.
